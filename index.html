About

The engineer behind the cells

I'm a robotics and automation engineer working primarily on FANUC and Mitsubishi cells for CNC machine tending, finishing, and general automation — usually as the person who owns a project from concept through commissioning and customer handoff. In practice that means the robot, the vision system, the safety architecture and the control strategy are my calls, and nothing goes to a customer without my sign-off on the code and the wiring.

Most of my work sits behind an NDA, which is why much of this portfolio is written rather than photographed. The exception is the two machines above: products exhibited publicly at IMTS 2024, where I did the robot programming, vision, integration, and operator screens, then stood on the show floor demonstrating them for a week. I went back for Cubic at IMTS 2026, demonstrating cobot and robot machine-tending systems and robotic post-processing and deburring.

What holds my attention is what decides whether a cell is still running a year later: recovery behavior, operator screens with few enough choices to stay safe, and changeover a customer's own people can do themselves.
