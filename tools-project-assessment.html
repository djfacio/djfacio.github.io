Professional Process Artifact

Robotic cell project assessment

A personal working tool for early automation scoping. It organizes the questions I want answered before design, quoting, risk review, controls architecture, and commissioning planning move too far downstream.

Why This Exists

Good robotic cells fail early or get expensive late when assumptions stay hidden.

This checklist turns those assumptions into visible engineering work: part variation, payload, vision strategy, robot-to-PLC behavior, safety, electrical requirements, recovery cases, and handoff documentation. It is not a replacement for a formal risk assessment, design review, or customer specification.
